Hello, my name is Gábor Kismárton. I'm currently a full-time physicist-engineering student at Budapest University of Technology and Economics in Hungary.

I'm part of three student clubs which take up most of my free time. In the IT club we manage the network infrastructure of the student residency that I live in, help the student with all kinds of IT related problems, provide internet for various university events and work on cool projects individually and together as well. In the "science club" (can't be translated to English), I help the economic working group write proposals to get equipment for experiments and finance educational trips and programmes for our members, and I also help organise these programmes. I'm also a part of an event organiser group for our faculty, where we organise all kinds of games and fun activities for the students in our faculty focused mainly on the integration of freshman students into the community. I also have a part-time job as an assistant editor for the Hungarian Chamber of Engineering.

My passion besides IT and the student clubs is working out; I go to the gym almost every day.
