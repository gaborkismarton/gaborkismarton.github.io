During this Hackathon my team and I set out to improve fraud detection with math for the company Seon, who specialize in anti-fraud technologies.

Our plan was to personalize the fraud detection process by fitting a polynomial to a person's transactional data and then later using this to detect deviations from the usual transactional activities. More details on the exact process can be found on my GitHub.

![Image](/assets/images/junction2022/ideating.jpg)

My main part was organizing our codebase, managing the GitHub repo whilst planning and editing our video. Since this was our first time creating a story for a project like this we did not really manage to convey that our idea was actually impressive, even though all the mentors from Seon liked the project.

![Image](/assets/images/junction2022/mentoring.jpg)

All together this was my first "real" Hackathon experience and probably the best so far. The venue, the food, the people, the mentoring sessions and the overall vibe of the event was fantastic.
