This time we embarked on a month-long coding competition involving tons of interesting and challenging problems that we had to solve.

The biggest challenge for us was definitely the bytecode limitation that the organizers enforced on us, so we had to write our solutions in a very optimized and crazy way utilizing code generation for unrolling loops and handling different cases many times during development.

We managed to convert all the information that we can detect to bitmaps and we implemented insanely effective convolutions on them.

For our macro strategy we turned to our economics and math knowledge to come up with a very innovative solution.

Sadly I cannot make the code public, nor share more details because then we could not utilize these ideas next time we attend this competition.
