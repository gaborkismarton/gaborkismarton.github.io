During this hackathon my team and I processed study material for medical students in order to create revision type study questions for the Hungarian startup Voovo, who have created a Duolingo-like app for medical students.

We used both AI (mainly OpenAI's o1 reasoning model) and algorithmic techniques to create the questions.
We first converted the pdf materials (lecture slides/notes, textbooks, etc...) to text only and we also extracted the images separately and used the o1 model to get descriptions for them.
After this we used the different element styling options in latex for determining what are the important expressions that we definitely want to include in our revision questions.

For the prompt we got a lot of help from the medical student in our team who already had experience with this type of material and knew what questions and wrong/distractor answers are actually good.

Overall we had lots of fun during this 48-hour event, we met cool people, ate a bunch of pizza and learned a ton about AI, their usage with apis and different solutions that already exist for this problem, like vector databases.
