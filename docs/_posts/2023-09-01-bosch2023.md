On this 24 hour Hackathon we set out on an ambitious project to engineer a drill that uses accelerometers and an ultrasonic distance sensor to drill precise depth holes and stop when a screw would be stripped.

![Image](/assets/images/bosch2023/drill.jpg)

My task during this competition was to design the circuit for the sensors and program our microcontroller of choice, a Raspberry Pi Pico. Due to having a faulty Pico I ended up wasting almost all of my time on trying to get the I2C interfaces to work, so I did not manage to develop much of the functionality that my teammates planned in a block diagram.

![Image](/assets/images/bosch2023/desk.jpg)
