### Project Idea
During the pandemic my high school couldn't organize it's usual Valentine's Day Secret message distribution so I was tasked with creating a system that could do this online, but it had to include a censorship feature.

### Descent into Madness

I had approximately one week to complete the project while having to attend online classes as well.
I wasn't given any money for hosting the app and finding a free solution proved very difficult. I encountered many of the free limitations early on, especially the email sending limits.
This was the first time I had to set up authentication and a persistent database.


### Conclusion

I learned a lot about writing a web app, python flask, hosting a web app, databases, authentication, mass email sending and the fact that I won't always be able to test my projects at a smaller scale.
